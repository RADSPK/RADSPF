📋 Pool Ledger
All lapsed benefits from ineligible employee settlements
How to use this pool
When an ineligible employee is settled, the employer's contribution share and any profit/loss allocated to that employee is moved here rather than being paid out. The pool balance can then be:
  • Returned to the organisation — transfer the amount back to the company bank account (record the date and transfer reference)
  • Used for adjustment — apply against any fund miscalculation, error correction, or internal reallocation (describe the purpose)
Each disposal is recorded with a date and reference for audit purposes.
